What is
corporate physical health & wellness

PREHAB Corporate Physical Health & Wellness is a proactive, evidence-based workplace health programme that integrates Physiotherapy, Nutrition, Strength & Conditioning, and Sports Massage Therapy to optimise the physical capacity, resilience, and long-term musculoskeletal health of employees within a corporate environment.

Unlike traditional occupational health models that respond to injury or illness after it occurs, PREHAB's corporate wellness approach is fundamentally preventive and performance-driven — identifying physical risk factors, movement deficiencies, and lifestyle-related health threats before they translate into pain, dysfunction, injury, or absenteeism.

How do work-related MSK health disorders affect your organisation?

Muscles or joint pains

MSK disorders strain not just muscles, but also the individual’s and company’s bottom line.

How PREHAB helps your team

Process — from first touch to goals achieved

All services can be delivered on-site, at PREHAB clinic centres, and online.

Complete Physical Health Check

  • Musculoskeletal screening for movement, stability, posture, and mobility analysis to design preventive rehab plans.
  • Tech-based performance testing
    1. Muscle meter to identify muscular strength asymmetries
    2. Force plates to identify movement weight distribution asymmetries

Technology

Muscle Meter

  1. The Muscle Meter is a versatile, pocket-sized, 3-in-1 handheld dynamometer (push, pull, and grip) used by professionals to measure force in nearly every muscle group.
  2. The device is designed to improve client outcomes by providing objective data, without slowing down workflows or requiring expensive, long-term contracts.

K-Invent Force Plates

  1. Kinvent force plates are portable, lightweight, high-precision tools designed for measuring balance, strength, and power in rehabilitation and athletic performance.
  2. The plates integrate with the Kinvent Physio App, allowing for data visualisation, real-time feedback, and personalized reporting.

Reporting System

  1. 1. The “Hardware”: Precision Assessment

    We don’t guess; we measure. Elite-grade sports science technology gives a digital blueprint of physical health.

    • Kinvent Force Plate: measures power symmetry and balance to identify overcompensation on one side.
    • MAT Muscle Meter: quantifies exact muscle strength and reveals weak links earlier.

  2. 2. The “Software”: Live Progress Architecture

    Reporting is a living document. Cloud-based S&C trackers log every rep, set, and recovery metric in one transparent system.

    With progressive loading, you can see real-time capability growth across 3, 6, and 12 months.

Requirements — on-site clinic

To ensure clinical precision and data-driven results, our team integrates into your workspace with a streamlined, professional setup.

  • Dedicated space: Private room (minimum 200 sq. ft.) for assessments and treatments.
  • Clinical setup: One physiotherapy bed and pillow for MSK evaluations and manual therapy.
  • Data station: Work desk with 3 power sockets for Kinvent sensors and computer setup.
  • Assessment hub: Desktop PC or laptop with standard office chair for real-time data logging.
  • Consultation area: Two chairs for intake and recovery discussions.
  • Hygiene & care: Wash basin with mirror for clinical standards and professional presentation.
