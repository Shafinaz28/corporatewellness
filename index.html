What is
corporate physical health & wellness

Corporate Physical Health & Wellness by PREHAB is a proactive, performance-driven health programme that keeps your workforce strong, capable, and present — before problems arise.

Rather than responding to injury and absence after the fact, PREHAB identifies physical risks early and addresses them through expert physiotherapy, nutrition, strength & conditioning, and sports massage — embedded directly into your workplace.

The result? Fewer sick days, lower healthcare costs, and a workforce that genuinely performs.

How PREHAB helps your team

Process — from first touch to goals achieved

All services can be delivered on-site, at PREHAB clinic centres, and online.

Complete Physical Health Check

  • Musculoskeletal screening for movement, stability, posture, and mobility analysis to design preventive rehab plans.
  • Tech-based performance testing
    1. Muscle meter to identify muscular strength asymmetries
    2. Force plates to identify movement weight distribution asymmetries

Requirements — on-site clinic

To ensure clinical precision and data-driven results, our team integrates into your workspace with a streamlined, professional setup.

  • Dedicated space: Private room (minimum 200 sq. ft.) for assessments and treatments.
  • Clinical setup: One physiotherapy bed and pillow for MSK evaluations and manual therapy.
  • Data station: Work desk with 3 power sockets for Kinvent sensors and computer setup.
  • Assessment hub: Desktop PC or laptop with standard office chair for real-time data logging.
  • Consultation area: Two chairs for intake and recovery discussions.
  • Hygiene & care: Wash basin with mirror for clinical standards and professional presentation.
